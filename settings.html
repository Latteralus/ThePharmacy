Settings

Game

The speed a new game starts at. In the game, change it with the top bar or the 1–4 keys.
Autosave
Saves your game in the background while you play, keeping each game's last three autosaves.
minutes
Minutes of play at 1× speed (1 to 60). Faster speeds save sooner; a paused game doesn't save.

Reports

Daily summary
Start the next day straight away. Warnings come as notifications, and past days stay on the Dashboard.
Monthly report
It opens after the last closing of each month. Skipped, it stays on the Dashboard and the Finances page.
Yearly report
It opens after the last closing of December. All three are also on the in-game Settings page.

Display

Makes everything larger or smaller. Applies when you save.
Fullscreen
Applies when you save.
